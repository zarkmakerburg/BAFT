# BAFT Tunnel → Sanaei/3x-ui Manual Inbound: Operator Contract

The operator explicitly chooses the foreign EX Xray inbound port (1–65535), or requests a cryptographically random suggestion from 20000–59999. A suggested port is checked against known active BAFT EX inventory, NOT live operating-system or external Xray listeners. The wizard maps it to BAFT target=127.0.0.1:PORT; the inter-server BAFT EX port is a distinct field.

After the BAFT plan has been reviewed and deployed, the operator creates an inbound at that exact port in Sanaei/3x-ui on EX. BAFT does not set up the Xray inbound, protocol/users, subscription, or public IR ingress. If clients connect via Iran, independently wire the IR public port to BAFT's IR local route; then manually enter the actual reachable IR IP/domain and client port into the appropriate Sanaei client configuration. Verify the full client → IR ingress → BAFT → EX Xray flow. A green tunnel is not evidence of a working Xray client path.

Approved urgent engine roadmap: (0) capability-typed, fail-closed engine driver contract and lifecycle; (1) FRP direct/reverse TCP adapter; (2) Rathole independent adapter; (3) independently evidenced WS/WSS and QUIC/UDP; (4) other TCP/UDP reverse, Backhaul/Reality/GRE/Hashem as verified standalone engines or accurately labelled compositions. No UI enabling until artifact supply-chain/licensing, two-node health and E2E, reboots, coexistence, failure rollback, port conflicts and secretless telemetry are proven. Current native BAFT remains the only deployable engine until those gates pass.
